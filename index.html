Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 April 2026

This privacy policy applies to the EAT2 app (referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, created by John Phua (referred to as "Service Provider") as a free service. This service is provided "AS IS".

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use the Application, we collect:

  • Email address — required to create and authenticate your account.
  • Display name and username — optional, set by you in your profile.
  • Bio — optional, set by you in your profile.
  • Profile photo — optional, chosen by you from your device's photo library.
  • Review content — the text, ratings, and details (meal type, spend, group size, wait time) you submit when reviewing a hawker stall.
  • Saved stalls and follows — which stalls you save and which users you follow within the app.
  • Stall submissions — details of any stalls you submit to the catalogue for review.

We also collect standard server-side information when you connect to our backend services, including your IP address and request timestamps. This is handled by Supabase, our database provider, and is used solely for operating and securing the service.

The Application does not collect your device's GPS or location. The Application does not use any advertising or analytics SDKs. No data is sold or shared with advertisers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Application (account management, displaying your reviews and profile).
  • To display your reviews and profile to other users of the Application.
  • To contact you with important service notices if necessary.

Third-Party Access

Your data is stored and processed by Supabase, our backend provider. Supabase adheres to industry-standard data protection practices. We do not share your personal information with any other third parties, except as required by law (e.g. in response to a valid legal request or subpoena).

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by uninstalling the Application using the standard uninstall process on your device or via the App Store.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as you have an active account. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly within the Application:

Profile → Delete Account

Deletion is immediate and irreversible — your reviews, profile, saved stalls, and all account data are removed. If you encounter any issues, contact us at John.daniel.pxy@gmail.com and we will respond promptly.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at John.daniel.pxy@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.
